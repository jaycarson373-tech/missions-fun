## Extended thesis

The best things players find rarely come from a checklist. A hidden turn. A perfect photo. A route nobody thought to take.

Missions turns those ideas into community briefs: a specific objective, a published reward budget and a checklist of evidence. Browse the board, choose a challenge and bring back proof. Hold at least 2.55M $MISSIONS to meet the token requirement; acceptance still depends on completing the brief.

Exploration, driving, photography and source-backed research each get their own place on the board. No mystery judging. No payout number without funding behind it. Every opened bounty should tell you what counts before you spend time on it.

The preview is available now. Its current briefs are proposals. Wallet checks, funded submissions, review and payout receipts are the next integration steps.

## Five launch-sequence posts

1. The map tells you where to go. The community gives you a reason. Missions starts with six proposed briefs across exploration, driving, photography and research. Open one. Read what counts. Save your next run.
2. 2,550,000 $MISSIONS. That is the holding requirement for bounty eligibility. It is not a prize guarantee. The brief still needs completing, the proof still needs reviewing, and the reward needs to be funded.
3. “Find the frame.” Take a shot from an official GTA VI trailer. Find the same spot in-game. Bring the reference, your route and your own footage. One of the community briefs we want to open after release.
4. Good bounties are specific: objective, deadline, reward, evidence. If a player cannot tell what counts before starting, the brief is not ready. That is the standard for the Missions board.
5. A claimed win is a sentence. A verified win has evidence. We are building the submission-to-review trail first, with payout receipts once funded missions open.
